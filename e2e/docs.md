# Noridoc: E2E Playtest

Path: @/e2e

### Overview

- Browser playtests cover user-facing park setup, ride access, save flows, mobile input, message controls, and offline startup. Tests drive the Vite app through Playwright, exercising the UI, worker-backed simulation, and save system together.
- Starter Park browser flows cover flat-ride setup, admission pricing, save-file round trips, and ride and stall access. Ride flows cover coaster and water rides, invalid-placement explanations, testing/opening, guest use, and demolition. Separate cases verify research gating, touch input, and offline startup; the year-one objective run remains opt-in.
- The P1 UI fidelity flow covers WebGL2 desktop window placement, coaster construction controls, paused construction, the 2× construction cap, ownership display and feedback, Land Rights purchase controls, and the ride-window preview.

### How it fits into the larger codebase

- The root `pnpm e2e` command uses [playwright.e2e.config.ts](../playwright.e2e.config.ts) and the shared [playwright.config.ts](../playwright.config.ts); it complements the shorter smoke suite.
- Browser tests send actions through the same UI and simulation worker path as a player. Scenario setup centers the camera on the park gate and its contiguous inward entrance path.
- Mobile profiles use Chromium device emulation and touch injection. They exercise pointer handling and viewport behavior, not Safari or physical-device performance.
- Playwright stores screenshots and traces as test artifacts; ride-access tests attach visual checkpoints to the report.

### Core Implementation

- [starter-playtest.spec.ts](starter-playtest.spec.ts) starts Starter Park, hires staff, places and completes a flat ride, sets park admission pricing, and creates, exports, imports, and loads a named save. Its path-building step targets the configured entrance continuation, checks the selected tool's location feedback, and verifies the exported map contains more paths than the initial entrance connection. It confirms staff and ride state after restoration.
- [starter-ride-access.spec.ts](starter-ride-access.spec.ts) runs ride-access flows on desktop and emulated Pixel 8. The shared access and demolition flow starts Starter Park, imports a test save that unlocks the Steel Looping Coaster, then builds and opens a flat ride, food stall, and coaster; this keeps coaster access coverage independent of Starter Park's normal research gate. It also verifies Log Flume test/open/guest use, demolishes a flat ride, stall, and coaster through their ride windows with refund confirmation, and checks desktop right-click / Pixel 8 long-press ride-window access. A separate fresh Starter Park test confirms the water-ride category remains locked until researched. Desktop and Pixel 8 coaster-over-water rejection flows also import the unlocked-coaster save before attempting track over open water. Other cases verify repeated ride types start entrance setup on the new ride and do not treat boarding tiles inside another ride as valid.
- [ride-access-repro.spec.ts](ride-access-repro.spec.ts) exercises a Ferris Wheel flow that imports a Starter Park save with the Ferris researched, keeping placement and access coverage independent of the default research gate; it then checks invalid placement explanations, testing/opening, guest use, and ride demolition through the UI. Its coaster flow covers track placement and access in the same end-to-end UI path.
- [art-scale.spec.ts](art-scale.spec.ts) imports a test save with the ride researched, prepares path access, and places a Swinging Ship and Top Spin on desktop and emulated Pixel 8. Paired screenshots show the placement preview and the same UI without that sprite; a fixed crop measures its visible area in CSS pixels. Each flow then completes and opens the ride. [people-scale.spec.ts](people-scale.spec.ts) starts Sandbox through the UI on desktop and Pixel 8, checks visible canvas detail, hires each staff role through the staff panel, and attaches park screenshots. [tools/sprites/generate.test.ts](../tools/sprites/generate.test.ts) checks generated people frame coverage, compact proportions, directional poses, and distinct staff role colors.
- [object-inspection-parts.spec.ts](object-inspection-parts.spec.ts) loads a staged save (coaster, bench, bin; [support/inspection-showcase.ts](support/inspection-showcase.ts)) and clicks coaster track, station, train, entrance and exit, then a bin and a vandalised bench (checks the popup stays on screen, Repair clears the vandalised state), on desktop and Pixel 8 under WebGL2 and Canvas2D.
- [object-inspection.spec.ts](object-inspection.spec.ts) checks scenery, rides, the park gate, guests and staff through Select-mode inspection on desktop and Pixel 8 in both renderers. Its flat-ride checks verify that body-cell targets cover the ride footprint and that the grouped hover outline includes the body slices and placed entrance and exit.
- [person-movement.spec.ts](person-movement.spec.ts) checks staff and guest pickup and relocation on desktop and Pixel 8 under WebGL2 and Canvas2D. The guest flow compares the held sprite height with its walking pick target at the same zoom (within 10%) and checks the ground shadow scales with zoom. A desktop WebGL2 test drags a hovered guest and checks the hover outline is hidden once the guest is held.
- [support/park.ts](support/park.ts) and [support/shots.ts](support/shots.ts) hold the shared Starter Park helpers: toolbar and minimap navigation, tile clicks, the shared footpath/ride geometry (`STARTER_PATH_TILES`, `FLAT_RIDE_ANCHOR`, ...), footprint and ring maths from the sim catalogue, in-page tile picking against the real terrain, and screenshot diff/crop helpers.
- [flat-ride-footprints.spec.ts](flat-ride-footprints.spec.ts) builds every flat ride type through the UI on a flat street site (ghost covers the footprint, entrance candidates equal the terrain-allowed ring, entrance and exit placed from the ring, ride opened, guests served). Swinging Ship, Ferris Wheel and Top Spin are also built at all four orientations, each build is screenshotted in all four view rotations, one pass runs under forced Canvas2D, and a Pixel 8 case checks the Rotate ride button clears the HUD. Set `FOOTPRINT_SHOTS_DIR` to write the JPEG sheets (committed under docs/playtest/assets/footprints) instead of the test output folder.
- [art-review.spec.ts](art-review.spec.ts) compares default-zoom facility renders against the same scene without the facility, requiring a minimum visible-pixel area in both backends, and captures a populated context scene in all rotations. It also builds completed coaster and transport rides through the simulation's `createCoaster` and `placeTrack` command path, then renders their snapshots with the production Canvas2D and WebGL2 renderers. It verifies that each placed track piece resolves to generated art with opaque pixels and that the rendered canvas has no red fallback marker; optional capture output includes inventory sheets and per-ride images. The fixture imports [sprite-pages.ts](../src/render/sprite-pages.ts) by its canonical module URL so lazy page loads are visible to the renderer's shared sprite store.
- [p1-ui-fidelity.spec.ts](p1-ui-fidelity.spec.ts) requires the desktop canvas to use WebGL2, checks populated ride windows avoid status bar and pointer overlap, validates compact coaster controls, builds while paused, checks the 2× construction cap and ride preview, verifies saved desktop window placement, and exercises ownership UI. The ride-access Pixel 8 scenario checks the construction controls fit the mobile viewport and uses touch to build.
- [scenario-selection.spec.ts](scenario-selection.spec.ts) checks scenario tabs and previews, plus persistence of completed-park status across reloads.
- [footpath-tool.spec.ts](footpath-tool.spec.ts) drives the Footpaths window through the UI: a dragged run previews its tiles and cost and lays them on release, a refused run shows red tiles with the reason, right-click and long-press remove a footpath with a refund, piece mode builds and removes at the cursor, and the hint shows once. Shared helpers are in [footpath-helpers.ts](footpath-helpers.ts).
- [wall-edge-placement.spec.ts](wall-edge-placement.spec.ts) exercises wall scenery through the browser tool in WebGL2 and forced Canvas2D: it places edge walls, rotates the camera, and drag-places a continuous fence run. It complements simulation tests for per-edge occupancy and wall-blocked path links.
- [footpath-terrain.spec.ts](footpath-terrain.spec.ts) raises a clear owned Starter Park tile and verifies that paths follow the resulting neighboring slopes. It projects seeded map tiles through the live camera, so entrance-centered openings and viewport changes do not make clicks land on unrelated tiles.
- [people-depth.spec.ts](people-depth.spec.ts) checks pixel ownership between a guest and flat, raised, queue and ramp paths, benches, trees and stalls, in WebGL2 and Canvas2D at all four rotations. It loads the dev-only [depth-fixture.html](../depth-fixture.html) ([src/dev/depth-fixture.ts](../src/dev/depth-fixture.ts)), which draws a hand-built map with one guest at a chosen position, and renders each case with and without the guest and on a bare map with the same camera. Pixels the guest owns are those of the bare-map render; pixels the scene owns are those of the scene-only render, so the check does not depend on the art. It asserts that a guest is not covered by the surface it stands on, is behind an object nearer the camera (only where the sprites overlap), and in front of one farther away.
- [rotation-ownership.spec.ts](rotation-ownership.spec.ts) checks two rotation contracts in the browser: a fixture park's placed objects own pixels at their projected anchors in both renderers, and a simulated wooden coaster circuit stays on its world tiles beside a path and stall in every view. The circuit case confirms a test train is present and captures all four camera rotations in Canvas2D and WebGL2.
- When enabled with `E2E_RUN_YEAR=1`, the final stage runs at 4× speed until the year-one outcome dialog appears and captures whether the objective was met or missed.
- Scenario startup centers the camera on the park gate and up to three contiguous inward entrance-path tiles; when entrance coordinates are unavailable, it falls back to the map center. The playtest derives the Starter Park continuation tile from its scenario definition so the path action stays aligned with the configured entrance.
- [land-tool.spec.ts](land-tool.spec.ts) drives the Land window on desktop and emulated Pixel 8 through the UI only: hover previews (corner, edge, tile, 3×3), price tooltip, refusals with reasons, tap, drag up and down, flatten, smooth, and checks the saved game against what the preview announced. Tiles are located by calibrating on the overlay diamond under the pointer rather than reading the camera; phones centre the camera below the target because the tool sheets cover the lower half of the full-screen canvas.
- [area-tools.spec.ts](area-tools.spec.ts) drives the Clear scenery and Water windows on desktop and emulated Pixel 8 through the UI only: a Clear sweep previews and removes trees and path additions for the tooltip's price, Clear refuses unowned land, and Water refuses to cover a footpath. Results are checked against the saved game and HUD cash, and before/after screenshots are written to [docs/screenshots/area-tools](../docs/screenshots/area-tools).
- [water-basin.spec.ts](water-basin.spec.ts) imports the staged terrain of [support/water-scenes.ts](support/water-scenes.ts) and, on desktop and Pixel 8, fills a dug-out basin into a flat lake up to its rim, checks the spill refusal, drains it with Lower, and drives drag-to-repeat. [water-frames.spec.ts](water-frames.spec.ts) is a capture tool for review frames (flat land, dip, lake bank, shore with paths and trees, slope, map edge, all four rotations); it only runs with `WATER_FRAMES_DIR` set and its output is never committed.
- [context-menu.spec.ts](context-menu.spec.ts) right-clicks Meadowbrook Green's prebuilt rides: in Select the browser menu is suppressed (`defaultPrevented`) and the ride window opens, and with the Patrol tool a right-click removes the patrol tile.
- [support/park.ts](support/park.ts) holds helpers shared by the Land and area tool specs and the context-menu spec: starting a scenario, building a local copy of the scenario's world to find tiles, centring the camera on a tile, calibrating tile positions from the overlay diamond, hovering until a preview appears, reading HUD cash, and loading the exported save.
- [mobile-input.spec.ts](mobile-input.spec.ts) checks path tap and drag painting, pan and pinch effects via changed picked tiles, rotation controls, capped backing-buffer scale, page zoom/scroll prevention, visibility pause, and viewport resizing. It pauses the simulation, exports and deserializes the current save to locate a guest in the viewport, then long-presses the guest with Path active and asserts Guest details open. It also long-presses a path tile to check mobile removal, verifies saved map paths, and records HUD FPS samples with 2,000 guests on emulated Pixel 8.
- [mobile-runtime.spec.ts](mobile-runtime.spec.ts) checks the manifest, service-worker control, and that the app can reload offline and start a progressing sandbox simulation. It disables Chromium's HTTP cache before taking the browser offline, so the reload exercises the service-worker cache.
- [deferred-ui.spec.ts](deferred-ui.spec.ts) covers worker inspection and orders, patrol editing, Options categories, desktop resizing, Ctrl+S screenshot download, and the classic scenario selector tabs, preview and objective. Mobile guest/path coverage checks guest details, queue/slope/removal controls including touch-and-hold removal, and 44 px targets.
- [message-controls.spec.ts](message-controls.spec.ts) runs on desktop and emulated Pixel 8. It creates guest milestone messages through the Options UI, checks ticker X and Q dismissal plus phone swipe, opens Recent Messages and clears it, then disables Guest milestones through Options > Messages and verifies the setting suppresses the next event. It also checks the Controls description and that unavailable Marketing and Research categories are not offered.
- When enabled with `E2E_RUN_YEAR=1`, the final stage runs at 4× speed until the year-one outcome dialog appears and captures whether the objective was met or missed.

### Things to Know

- Scenario startup centers the camera on the park gate and up to three contiguous inward entrance-path tiles; when entrance coordinates are unavailable, it falls back to the map center. The playtest derives the Starter Park continuation tile from its scenario definition so the path action stays aligned with the configured entrance.
- `pnpm e2e` runs against the Vite development server and does not build the app. The Starter Park save round-trip completes without advancing a full year; set `E2E_RUN_YEAR=1` to opt into the longer year-one objective outcome run.
- Repository screenshots captured by the UI flow are stored in [docs/screenshots](../docs/screenshots). The Starter Park ride-access runs attach their screenshots to the Playwright report; those attachment files are test artifacts rather than committed repository assets. P1 construction and ride-window before/after images are committed in [docs/playtest/assets](../docs/playtest/assets).
- Mobile profiles and SwiftShader are browser emulation; their screenshots and FPS samples verify the browser integration path but do not characterize physical phone GPU performance.
- [queue-lines.spec.ts](queue-lines.spec.ts) runs on desktop and emulated Pixel 8 against Open Grounds, building everything through the controls (a walkway, a Merry-Go-Round with its entrance and exit, the queue line dragged out from the highlighted entrance, and an exit path). It reads the live sim through the dev-only `window.__sim` hook, spawns a crowd from the Game options menu, and checks that the prompt opens Footpaths with Queue line on, that the ghost says the line links to the entrance and the path, that guests queue one per tile in joining order, that wanderers never stand on a queue tile, and that a queue length limit of 3 caps the line and turns guests away with the "queue is too long" thought. Phones place paths with CDP touch drags.
- [mobile.spec.ts](mobile.spec.ts) runs at a 390 px phone viewport with touch: it checks the scrolling toolbar, stacked bottom sheets, a 44 px minimum for every visible control, and tap-to-build.
