# Decisions

Resolutions of the cross-spec conflicts listed in `docs/mechanics/README.md`. Constants marked _tunable_ live in code as named constants and are calibrated in playtesting.

| Topic                         | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tick rate                     | 40 ticks/s (`TICK_RATE`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| World model                   | Tile element stacks per tile (engine spec). Track uses the same tile grid; sub-tile geometry belongs to track pieces, not the map.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Calendar                      | 16,384 ticks per month, 8 months per year (March-October). Tunable.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Wages                         | Accrue weekly; reported monthly in the finance report.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Excitement penalty            | ×0.75 per intensity threshold exceeded. Tunable.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Park rating                   | Integer, clamped 0-999. Formula per economy spec including the ride terms; calibrated in [BALANCE.md](BALANCE.md).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Food/drink seeking            | A hungry/thirsty guest heads to a known stall if it has seen one; otherwise wanders and buys on encounter.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Capacities                    | 8,192 guest slots; ride and entity ids are 32-bit. The 5,000-guest mixed-staff-and-litter benchmark must stay at or under 3 ms/tick at p99.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Park rating happiness         | Penalty `500 - 2 x min(250, 3 x happy%)` per the rating-formula guide; a guest is happy above 50% happiness. Uses whole-percent inputs, so an empty park takes no penalty (the guide leaves the zero-guest case open). _Tunable._                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Difficult rating              | Scenario flag `difficultRating` (default off) starts the rating from 1,050 instead of 1,150. Stored in the ledger, hashed, saved (additive, default off).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Rating terms not yet modelled | Casualties use 100 per casualty (guide: 25 per drowning, 200 per fatal crash, with decay) until per-type inputs exist. Park value keeps the ride-value-plus-$7-per-guest placeholder until rides expose five-minute rider counts and type bonuses; the duplicate-type 25% reduction waits for the same work.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Loans and interest            | Keep simple weekly interest: annual basis points x balance / 32 weeks, floored, charged on the weekly boundary. Repayment is immediate and limited by cash. The guide does not settle simple vs compound, the sampled balance, or rounding; these wait for a controlled RCT2 observation. Loan steps are $1,000.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Company value                 | Park value + cash - loan (strategy guide). Already implemented; no change.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Marketing                     | Six campaign families, ids 0-5 in manual order (ids 0 and 1 unchanged). Durations 1-6 weeks, one running campaign, replacement on start. Weekly prices and attraction multipliers are unsourced hypotheses (the manual transcriptions disagree): free park-entry $50, park advertising $150 (unchanged), free ride vouchers $75 and selected-ride advertising $80 (ride target), half-price entry $60, free food or drink $40 (item target). The ride or item target is stored; targeted campaigns still give the park-wide bonus until the guests workstream applies per-target effects. Voucher fulfilment is not modelled. _Tunable._ |
| Research funding              | Funding is charged monthly, $0 / $100 / $200 / $400, on the month boundary (before the rollover). Progress accrues weekly: 21 / 28 / 42 work units at minimum / normal / maximum, that is 3 / 4 / 6 units a day over seven days. An invention of 180 units takes 60 days at minimum, 45 at normal and 30 at maximum, as the reference reports. Costs are scenario-set in work units. _Tunable._                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Research order                | Empty category mask pauses progress while funding is still charged (the manual's reading). The community fallback to remaining categories is not adopted. Player priority is an ordered item list; unranked items follow scenario order; unknown or repeated ids are rejected.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Calendar and month length     | Sim weeks are treated as seven days, four weeks to a month, so 60/45/30-day intervals map to 8.6/6.4/4.3 weeks. The mechanics note's 32-day month conflicts with this and is open. Display day labels stay 30/31 days. Same-tick order: research, then the economy's weekly charges, then fortnightly upkeep, monthly rollover and the rating refresh.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Scenario objectives           | Judged once, on the first tick at or after the deadline (kept). Monthly income reads the last completed month. Other goal timings wait for scenario-by-scenario RCT2 checks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Awards                        | Deferred: no award catalogue, lifecycle or effect exists yet. The guide's four-month duration and 1.25 / 0.75 multipliers are unverified.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Starter rating target         | 650 (was 730). Under the happiness term modest and ambitious builds overlap; at 650 they win 10 and 12 of 12 seeds, and at 670 the highest target that keeps both at 9 or more. The scenario's own seed (2026) ends at 653, a three-point margin. See [BALANCE.md](BALANCE.md#starter-objective).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Save format                   | Version 10. Research saves from version 9 and older store costs and progress in cents, which convert at the old minimum rate (2,500 cents a week = 21 units), so each invention keeps its duration at minimum funding. `difficultRating`, `campaignTarget` and research `priority` are additive and default when absent (no version bump).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

## Parallel work conventions

- Each workstream owns its module directory under `src/sim/<module>` (or `src/render`, `src/ui`). Edits to `src/sim/core` (World, Command union, constants, `SYSTEM_ORDER`) must be small and additive; rebase on `main` before opening a PR.
- Every PR keeps `pnpm lint`, `format:check`, `typecheck`, `test`, `build` and `bench --guests 5000` green, and adds determinism tests (same seed, same `hashWorld`).
- Sessions cannot push `.github/workflows/`; ask the coordinator for CI changes.
