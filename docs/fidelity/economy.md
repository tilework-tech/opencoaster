# Economy, Scenario Goals, and Calendar Fidelity

This document records the behavior OpenCoaster needs to match for the original _RollerCoaster Tycoon 2_ (RCT2) management and scenario systems. It is a clean-room behavioral specification: prose and tables only, with no copied implementation, assets, or data. Values are labeled by evidence strength because the RCT2 manual explains player-facing rules but does not publish most internal formulas. OpenRCT2-derived analysis is useful compatibility evidence, not independent proof that every edge case matches the original executable.

## Evidence labels

- **Manual-confirmed:** stated or shown in the RCT2 manual.
- **Behavior reference:** an exact value or rule described by a public RCT2 community guide or OpenRCT2 compatibility reference. Treat it as a target hypothesis until checked against RCT2 itself.
- **Unsettled:** sources conflict, omit a required detail, or do not establish original-game behavior.

### Park rating

The rating is an integer from 0 through 999. It combines guest count, guest happiness, lost guests, ride availability and ratings, litter, and fatalities. The detailed constants below come from a guide explicitly derived from OpenRCT2 0.1.2; they are strong behavioral clues, not verified measurements of every RCT2 release.

| Component             | Reported rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Base and cap          | Start from 1,150 points, or 1,050 in a scenario with difficult rating, then clamp the final result to 0–999.                                                                                                                                              | Behavior reference: [park-rating formula guide](https://forums.openrct2.org/topic/2798-guide-to-calculate-park-rating/).                                                                       |
| Guests                | Cap the guest count used by this term at 2,000. The adjustment is the integer guest count divided by 13, minus 150, giving a range from −150 to +3.                                                                                                       | Same formula guide.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Happiness             | A guest is counted as happy above 50% happiness. The penalty starts at 500. The reported offset is twice the smaller of 250 and the integer result of 300 times happy guests divided by all guests. Thus roughly 83.34% happy guests remove this penalty. | Same formula guide. Exact integer operation order and the zero-guest case are not independently verified for RCT2.                                                                             |
| Lost guests           | Guests who are lost and trying to leave have no penalty for the first 25; each additional such guest costs 7 rating points. The guide reports no separate cap.                                                                                            | Same formula guide.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Ride uptime           | Average ride downtime can cost up to 200 points; zero downtime costs none.                                                                                                                                                                                | Same formula guide.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Ride ratings          | Average excitement and intensity are compared with 3.68 and 5.20, respectively; each average term costs up to 50 points. Total excitement and total intensity each have an 80.00 threshold; each total term costs up to 100 points below that threshold.  | Same formula guide.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Litter and casualties | Litter costs 4 points per eligible piece, capped at 600. The guide reports 25 points per drowning and 200 per fatal ride crash, with a casualty penalty that later decays; the total reported cap is 1,000.                                               | Same formula guide. The exact decay schedule is not established here.                                                                                                                          |
| Refresh timing        | OpenRCT2 compatibility notes use a 40 Hz simulation and the current OpenCoaster design refreshes rating every 512 ticks (12.8 seconds at 1×). The RCT2 manual does not establish this internal refresh interval.                                          | [OpenRCT2 date/time notes](https://github-wiki-see.page/m/Sadret/openrct2-plugin-wiki/wiki/Date-and-Time); cadence remains an implementation-derived target, not a manual-confirmed RCT2 fact. |

### Park value

The RCT2 manual describes park value as reflecting land and equipment, ride quality and profitability, and overall public reputation. A community formula guide derived from OpenRCT2 describes the compatibility calculation as the sum of ride contributions plus 7 currency units per guest in the park. A ride contribution depends on its current value, guest rides during the preceding five minutes, and a ride-type bonus; current value reflects excitement, intensity, nausea, and age. The guide reports a 25% reduction for additional rides of the same type.

The guide reports each ride's value contribution as its current ride value multiplied by the sum of recent riders and four times that ride type's bonus, with the reported duplicate-type reduction applied to additional rides of a type. It describes the current ride value as a rating-based value adjusted by age. The public guide's worked example contains an arithmetic inconsistency: its displayed multiplication evaluates to 510,400 before the stated division, while its prose reports a contribution of 51,400. Do not encode that example as a canonical test vector. The exact ride-value coefficients, aging table, throughput window boundaries, duplicate counting, integer operation order, and currency rounding need verification against RCT2.

Sources: [RCT2 manual, p. 50](https://rct2.rctspace.com/rct2_manual.pdf); [park-value formula guide](https://forums.openrct2.org/topic/2876-guide-how-to-calculate-park-value/). The formula guide identifies OpenRCT2 source as its basis.

### Marketing

The player can run one of six campaign families, choose a duration from 1 to 6 weeks, and pays a weekly price for each active week. Four campaigns promise an offer to guests; the other two advertise the whole park or a selected ride. Voucher campaigns can disappoint guests when the advertised offer is not actually honored.

| Campaign family                | Original-facing effect                          | Exact weekly cost / guest-conversion effect                                     |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free park-entry vouchers       | Invites prospective guests with free admission. | Conflicting manual transcriptions and implementation-derived tables; unsettled. |
| Free ride vouchers             | Promotes free rides on a selected ride.         | Unsettled.                                                                      |
| Half-price park-entry vouchers | Promotes half-price admission.                  | Unsettled.                                                                      |
| Free food or drink vouchers    | Promotes a selected complimentary item.         | Unsettled.                                                                      |
| Park advertising               | Increases awareness of the park.                | Unsettled.                                                                      |
| Selected-ride advertising      | Promotes a selected ride.                       | Unsettled.                                                                      |

Manual transcriptions disagree on campaign prices, and compatibility-source prices may have changed by version. Do not treat the current OpenRCT2 cost table or community-reported attraction multipliers as original RCT2 constants without a controlled comparison. The six campaign types and 1–6 week duration are documented by the [RCT2 manual, pp. 48–51](https://rct2.rctspace.com/rct2_manual.pdf); the visible duration choices are also listed in the [game strings reference](https://github-wiki-see.page/m/openrct2/openrct2/wiki/Strings-used-in-the-game). Conflicting price evidence is recorded in [the existing economy research](../mechanics/economy.md#marketing).

### Awards

Awards are assessed at month starts after a park meets eligibility conditions; in RCT2, no more than one new award is given at a month start. The RCT2 award set includes positive and negative assessments. The catalog includes, among others, Best Gentle Rides, Best Custom-Designed Rides, and Most Dazzling Ride Color Schemes; Most Confusing Park Layout is a negative RCT2 award. Several earlier awards also appear in RCT2. Eligibility depends on park conditions such as ride counts, ratings, guest thoughts, cleanliness, food, staff, and value.

The complete awards reference says an award remains for 5 months. An older OpenRCT2-derived guide describes 4 months and reports guest-generation multipliers of 1.25 for a positive award and 0.75 for a negative award. Treat duration and attraction effects as unsettled until directly checked in RCT2. Do not combine expansion-only awards into the RCT2 catalog.

Sources: [complete awards list, with RCT2-specific additions identified](https://rct.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Complete_Awards_List); [OpenRCT2-derived award criteria and effects](https://forums.openrct2.org/topic/2732-guide-how-to-win-awards-with-your-park-in-openrct2/).

### Scenario objectives

The scenario editor supports goals involving guest counts, park value, park rating, paying off the loan, monthly income/profit, and building rides that satisfy construction or rating requirements. A goal may combine conditions and include a deadline. The date display exists to let players track those deadlines. The manual's objective editor example is “number of guests at a given date” (p. 62); its text also names guest-count, park-value, and coaster-length goals. The game strings reference provides additional labels, including monthly ride-ticket income, qualifying coaster counts/lengths, completing partially built coasters, repaying a loan while reaching park value, and monthly food/merchandise profit.

The manual establishes the player-facing objective/deadline model, but the sources reviewed do not settle whether every goal is checked continuously, at a month boundary, or only at its deadline; nor do they establish all tie-breaking and terminal-state rules. Treat exact check timing and edge conditions as open until scenario-by-scenario RCT2 observations exist.

Sources: [RCT2 manual, date section p. 21 and objective editor p. 62](https://rct2.rctspace.com/rct2_manual.pdf); [objective and marketing strings reference](https://github-wiki-see.page/m/openrct2/openrct2/wiki/Strings-used-in-the-game).
