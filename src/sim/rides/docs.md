# Noridoc: Rides

Path: @/src/sim/rides

### Overview

- Owns ride type definitions, per-ride simulation state, operating cycles, ratings/value queries, and maintenance hooks.
- The catalog contains original placeholder definitions for four flat rides, two track-based coasters (Wooden Coaster, Steel Looping Coaster) and four service stalls/facilities; all art and tuning are clean-room work.
- Coasters are buildable and operable end to end: track placement, test run, entrance/exit/queue, open, train dispatch and guest boarding.
- Ride state is deterministic integer data. No original game assets, decompiled code, or copied OpenRCT2 source belongs in this module.

### How it fits into the larger codebase

- Ride instances live in `World.rides`, a struct-of-arrays `EntityStore` defined by [ride-store.ts](ride-store.ts) and created by [core/world.ts](../core/world.ts). The core state hash includes this store through [core/hash.ts](../core/hash.ts). [save/save.ts](../../save/save.ts) writes ride columns in save version 4 and migrates version 3 rides by replacing only the legacy ride schema segment, preserving allocator IDs and surrounding track arrays. Migrated rides remain closed until entrance/exit placement and queue connection are configured.
- [ride-system.ts](ride-system.ts) runs in the ordered simulation tick through [core/simulation.ts](../core/simulation.ts). It advances ride phases, age, reliability wear, and breakdown downtime using the world's seeded PRNG.
- Placement accepts tile coordinates from callers and checks them against [map/tile-map.ts](../map/tile-map.ts). Queue validation checks each post-entrance tile against the map's `Surface.Path`, and [guest behavior](../guests/docs.md) walks guests to the entrance over the footpath network (queue tiles are ordinary path elements with the queue flag, accepted as `Surface.Path` or `Surface.Queue`).
- The track workstream owns layouts ([track](../track/docs.md)) and [vehicles](../vehicles/docs.md) own train physics and test-run metrics. [coaster-system.ts](coaster-system.ts) (ordered right after `vehicles` in `SYSTEM_ORDER`) bridges them for rides whose type has `usesTrack`; [ride-system.ts](ride-system.ts) skips those rides. [ride-operations.ts](ride-operations.ts) still exposes the validated measured-stat interface.
- Coaster commands (`placeRideEntrance`, `placeRideExit`, `setRideStatus` with `open` / `close` / `test`) are routed from [core/simulation.ts](../core/simulation.ts) to [ride-commands.ts](ride-commands.ts). `createRide` and these commands report a `RideResult` ([ride-results.ts](ride-results.ts)) through `world.feedback`.
- [staff](../staff/docs.md) owns staff entities and will own job selection. Rides expose mechanic inspection, repair, and inspection-due hooks, but staff movement and automatic maintenance dispatch are not integrated.

### Core Implementation

- [queue-system.ts](queue-system.ts) (`ride-queue`, ordered right after `rides`) detects the tick a ride dispatches (`phase` just became `Loading`, or `Running` when `loadTicks` is 0, with `phaseTicks` still at its starting value) and seats up to `boarded` guests in `Waiting` for that ride as `Riding`, charging the ticket (only when the park is in per-ride pricing) to `RideTickets`. `ticketPriceCents` is the shared price rule. Stalls and facilities never queue: guests use them at their entrance tile.
- `setRideOpen` requires only a placed entrance for stalls and facilities (`isShopCategory`); other rides still need an exit and a connected queue.

- [ride-types.ts](ride-types.ts) holds immutable `RIDE_CATALOG` tuning for the flat rides, stalls and coasters. Coaster entries have category `coaster`, `usesTrack`, an empty footprint (the ride occupies no map tile; its track does), capacity 12 (wooden) / 16 (steel), base ratings added to measured ones, and `trackRules` (wooden: 40 levels, no steep slopes, inversions or launch; steel: 80 levels, all allowed) enforced by the track command handler.
- [coaster.ts](coaster.ts) has `startRideTest`, `abortRideTest` and `invalidateRideTest` (called on any track change).
- [ride-commands.ts](ride-commands.ts): entrance/exit are placed on a tile adjacent to a station footprint tile, place queue/path tiles and charge the path cost; the queue is auto-linked by walking `Queue` tiles to an adjacent `Path` tile. `open` requires a passed test plus entrance, exit and connected queue; `close` and `test` are also handled here.
- [coaster-system.ts](coaster-system.ts), per tick for each `usesTrack` ride:
  - Testing: collects the `TestComplete` train stats via `recordRideTestMeasurements`, sets ratings to measured plus base ratings. A stall, crash or 12,000-tick timeout is a failed test.
  - Open: runs up to `min(4, maxTrainsOnTrack)` trains: one per block-section boundary beyond the first (see [vehicles](../vehicles/docs.md#block-zones)), so a plain circuit runs one train and each placed block brake adds one. The first train spawns at the platform; another spawns whenever the station zone is clear and the count is short. For the train standing in the station, an empty train with a non-empty queue waits for the minimum wait and a full-enough queue or the maximum wait, then riders are seated at once (`seatQueuedGuests` in [queue-system.ts](queue-system.ts), which `rideQueueSystem` no longer does for track rides), counted off `queueLength` and added to `boarded`. The train leaves after `loadTicks` once the zone beyond the station is clear. A train that arrives brakes in (state `Arriving`), stops at the platform end and unloads for `unloadTicks` (`Unloading`); only then are its riders released and the trip counted (`completeCycle`). An empty train that blocks a loaded one waiting behind it leaves empty to make room. A stalled or crashed train, or one out for more than 20,000 ticks (not counting time held at a block boundary), sets the ride Broken (`breakDownRide`). Closed rides let trains finish their lap and unload, then remove them. A `close` command on a Broken ride is refused with `Broken`: a broken coaster keeps its test, and mechanics repair it. The ride's `phase` is derived each tick from the trains (Running > Unloading > Loading > Waiting).
  - Riders on several trains: a boarding guest stores `cyclesCompleted + loadedTrips`, the number of trips that must finish before theirs; trips finish in departure order on one circuit, and `tripFinished` in the guest system compares with wrap-around. `abortCycle` (track edit, breakdown, reset) must run before the trains are removed so every loaded trip is released.
- [coaster-fixtures.ts](coaster-fixtures.ts) holds test circuits (e.g. `SMALL_CIRCUIT`) shared by tests. Per-instance columns in [ride-store.ts](ride-store.ts) hold placement, queue occupancy, phase timers, ratings, prices, age, reliability, and optional track-test measurements.
- [ride-operations.ts](ride-operations.ts) validates ride creation, entrance/exit placement, queue-chain input, operating options, queue counts, prices, and measured stats. Entrance/exit placement, operating configuration, and reopening are blocked while a ride is loading, running, or unloading, including when it has been closed or broken during that cycle. Reopening during an active cycle leaves its phase timer and boarded riders intact. Queue reconnection is rejected while the ride is Open/Waiting and, even after closing, when the proposed internal-tile capacity is below retained queue occupancy. Closing preserves queue occupancy. Reconnection is otherwise allowed after closing when no active cycle phase remains. Opening requires placed, distinct entrance and exit coordinates plus a connected queue; track-based types also require a passed test (`tested`).
- Version 3 save migration preserves ride allocator IDs and copies each ride's type, entrance coordinates, ratings, price, queue count, reliability, and breakdown counter. Migrated rides start closed because that format did not store entrance/exit placement flags or queue connection data; callers must configure entrance and exit placement and reconnect the queue before opening. The migration replaces the old ride schema segment while retaining the serialized track arrays that follow it.
- Queue input is a caller-supplied ordered chain with at least two coordinates: the entrance followed by one or more tiles. The module checks bounds, Manhattan adjacency, repeats, entrance alignment, and requires every tile after the entrance—including the endpoint—to be `Surface.Path`. It does not verify that the endpoint connects to the public path network. It stores only the count of internal queue tiles (excluding the entrance and endpoint) and a connection flag. Capacity is four guests per internal queue tile; guest identities and full queue geometry are not retained.
- Open rides dispatch from `Waiting` when the minimum wait has elapsed and either the configured load fraction or maximum wait is reached. Dispatch removes up to ride capacity from queue occupancy and records riders in the active cycle, then enters `Loading` for the configured load duration before `Running`. A zero `loadTicks` duration skips directly to `Running`. Riders count as served only after the run and unload phases complete. Throughput is a theoretical hourly rate from capacity and the configured load, run, and unload durations; estimated wait is queue length divided by approximate service rate.
- Flat-ride excitement, intensity, and nausea are authored in hundredths and remain unchanged as the ride ages. Ride value uses those ratings and loses five percentage points of its rating-adjusted base value per age month, down to 40% after twelve months; stalls and facilities have zero ride value. Price feedback compares the configured price to that current value.
- The track-test interface stores duration, speed, signed fixed-point G measurements, drops, highest drop, and inversions. Reliability loses one point every 32 completed cycles; at reliability 10 or lower, cycle completion can trigger a seeded probabilistic breakdown. Downtime accrues while broken. A mechanic inspection raises reliability by 25, records the inspection tick, and returns a broken ride to service when its queue remains connected.

### Things to Know

- Time and operating defaults are integer ticks. A month is 16,384 ticks; catalog inspection intervals are one in-game hour at 40 ticks per second. New rides start with a minimum wait equal to authored load ticks, a maximum wait of four times that value, and a 128/255 load threshold.
- Queue validation requires an entrance coordinate plus at least one path tile, checks every post-entrance tile for `Surface.Path`, and checks caller-provided chain geometry. It does not retain the chain or prove that it connects to the wider park through pathfinding.
- Dispatch uses minimum wait, maximum wait, capacity, and load threshold. After dispatch, a positive `loadTicks` value drives a distinct `Loading` countdown before the run; zero skips loading. Stall service uses the same queue and cycle machinery with capacity one.
- Closing a ride during `Loading`, `Running`, or `Unloading` prevents another dispatch but lets the current cycle finish before the phase becomes `Closed`. A breakdown during an active cycle follows the same completion behavior, then leaves the ride in `Broken`; a breakdown while waiting changes phase immediately. Served-guest totals update after unload, including when the ride was closed or broke during that cycle.
- Entrance/exit placement and operating configuration reject edits during `Loading`, `Running`, or `Unloading`, even if the ride's state has already changed to `Closed` or `Broken`. Queue reconnection is additionally rejected while the ride state is `Open` (including `Waiting`), and rejects any proposed line whose internal-tile capacity is below retained occupancy, including after closing. Closing preserves occupancy, so a closed ride can only reconnect to a line with sufficient capacity. Queue reconnection otherwise becomes available after closing when no active cycle remains; a broken ride remains broken until inspected.
- `setRideOpen(..., false)` preserves the `Broken` ride state whenever either the stored state or phase is `Broken`. This also covers a breakdown during an active cycle: the ride state becomes broken immediately while its loading, running, or unloading phase finishes. `setRideOpen(..., true)` rejects broken rides and all active cycle phases (`Loading`, `Running`, and `Unloading`), so an open request cannot reset a cycle or strand boarded riders. A valid mechanic inspection restores reliability and changes a broken ride to `Open`/`Waiting` only when its queue is connected; without a connected queue it becomes `Closed`/`Closed`.
- Inspection-due checks and manual mechanic inspection are hooks, not scheduled staff behavior. Inspection accepts a mechanic only when the ride is not in its loading, running, or unloading phase; it can be inspected while waiting or closed. Inspection restores up to 25 reliability points, capped at 100. Repair duration or staff travel is not simulated here.
- `recordRideTestMeasurements` validates and persists measurements but does not itself derive ratings; the coaster system adds the ride type's base ratings to the vehicle-computed values. Ratings are additive per type, with no per-type coefficients.
- A coaster accepts one station piece only (a second is `PieceNotAllowed`). `loadTicks` is the time a loaded train waits in the station before leaving and `unloadTicks` the time it stands unloading. The number of trains is not a stored setting: it follows from the block brakes on the circuit, so the save format is unchanged. Any track edit clears `tested`, so the ride must be re-tested before opening. Breakdowns and crashes do not clear `tested`.
- Guest ride choice (tolerance * 2 rule in guest scoring) rejects most guests for intensities near 4.8, so natural demand for high-intensity coasters is low.
- Guests, pathfinding, staff automation, rendering, and ride-map occupancy remain integration responsibilities of their respective modules.
