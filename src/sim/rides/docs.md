# Noridoc: Rides

Path: @/src/sim/rides

### Overview

- Owns ride type definitions, per-ride simulation state, operating cycles, ratings/value queries, and maintenance hooks.
- The catalog contains original placeholder definitions for four flat rides and four service stalls/facilities; all art and tuning are clean-room work.
- Ride state is deterministic integer data. No original game assets, decompiled code, or copied OpenRCT2 source belongs in this module.

### How it fits into the larger codebase

- Ride instances live in `World.rides`, a struct-of-arrays `EntityStore` defined by [ride-store.ts](ride-store.ts) and created by [core/world.ts](../core/world.ts). The core state hash includes this store through [core/hash.ts](../core/hash.ts). [save/save.ts](../../save/save.ts) writes ride columns in save version 4 and migrates version 3 rides by replacing only the legacy ride schema segment, preserving allocator IDs and surrounding track arrays. Migrated rides remain closed until entrance/exit placement and queue connection are configured.
- [ride-system.ts](ride-system.ts) runs in the ordered simulation tick through [core/simulation.ts](../core/simulation.ts). It advances ride phases, age, reliability wear, and breakdown downtime using the world's seeded PRNG.
- Placement accepts tile coordinates from callers and checks them against [map/tile-map.ts](../map/tile-map.ts). Queue validation checks each post-entrance tile against the map's `Surface.Path`, but [pathfinding](../pathfinding/docs.md) and [guest behavior](../guests/docs.md) do not yet route guests through queues or board them automatically.
- The track workstream owns layouts and measured coaster behavior. [ride-operations.ts](ride-operations.ts) exposes a validated measured-stat interface for that integration; it records measurements but does not calculate track ratings. Current catalog entries are flat rides and stalls, so none require track testing to open.
- [staff](../staff/docs.md) owns staff entities and will own job selection. Rides expose mechanic inspection, repair, and inspection-due hooks, but staff movement and automatic maintenance dispatch are not integrated.

### Core Implementation

- [ride-types.ts](ride-types.ts) holds immutable `RIDE_CATALOG` tuning for Merry-Go-Round, Swinging Ship, Ferris Wheel, Twist, Food Stall, Drink Stall, Information Kiosk, and Toilets. Per-instance columns in [ride-store.ts](ride-store.ts) hold placement, queue occupancy, phase timers, ratings, prices, age, reliability, and optional track-test measurements.
- [ride-operations.ts](ride-operations.ts) validates ride creation, entrance/exit placement, queue-chain input, operating options, queue counts, prices, and measured stats. Entrance/exit placement and operating configuration are blocked while a ride is running or unloading, including when it has been closed or broken during that cycle. Queue reconnection is rejected while the ride is Open/Waiting and, even after closing, when the proposed internal-tile capacity is below retained queue occupancy. Closing preserves queue occupancy. Reconnection is otherwise allowed after closing when no run or unload phase is active. Opening requires placed, distinct entrance and exit coordinates plus a connected queue; track-based types would also require recorded test data.
- Version 3 save migration preserves ride allocator IDs and copies each ride's type, entrance coordinates, ratings, price, queue count, reliability, and breakdown counter. Migrated rides start closed because that format did not store entrance/exit placement flags or queue connection data; callers must configure entrance and exit placement and reconnect the queue before opening. The migration replaces the old ride schema segment while retaining the serialized track arrays that follow it.
- Queue input is a caller-supplied ordered chain with at least two coordinates: the entrance followed by one or more tiles. The module checks bounds, Manhattan adjacency, repeats, entrance alignment, and requires every tile after the entrance—including the endpoint—to be `Surface.Path`. It does not verify that the endpoint connects to the public path network. It stores only the count of internal queue tiles (excluding the entrance and endpoint) and a connection flag. Capacity is four guests per internal queue tile; guest identities and full queue geometry are not retained.
- Open rides dispatch from `Waiting` when the minimum wait has elapsed and either the configured load fraction or maximum wait is reached. Boarding removes up to ride capacity from queue occupancy and records riders in the active cycle. Riders count as served only after the run and unload phases complete. Throughput is a theoretical hourly rate from capacity and configured load/run/unload durations; estimated wait is queue length divided by approximate service rate.
- Flat-ride excitement, intensity, and nausea are authored in hundredths and remain unchanged as the ride ages. Ride value uses those ratings and loses five percentage points of its rating-adjusted base value per age month, down to 40% after twelve months; stalls and facilities have zero ride value. Price feedback compares the configured price to that current value.
- The track-test interface stores duration, speed, signed fixed-point G measurements, drops, highest drop, and inversions. Reliability loses one point every 32 completed cycles; at reliability 10 or lower, cycle completion can trigger a seeded probabilistic breakdown. Downtime accrues while broken. A mechanic inspection raises reliability by 25, records the inspection tick, and returns a broken ride to service when its queue remains connected.

### Things to Know

- Time and operating defaults are integer ticks. A month is 16,384 ticks; catalog inspection intervals are one in-game hour at 40 ticks per second. New rides start with a minimum wait equal to authored load ticks, a maximum wait of four times that value, and a 128/255 load threshold.
- Queue validation requires an entrance coordinate plus at least one path tile, checks every post-entrance tile for `Surface.Path`, and checks caller-provided chain geometry. It does not retain the chain or prove that it connects to the wider park through pathfinding.
- Cycle dispatch currently uses minimum wait, maximum wait, capacity, and load threshold. `loadTicks` contributes to defaults and throughput estimates; the phase machine does not run a distinct loading countdown. Stall service uses the same queue and cycle machinery with capacity one.
- Closing a ride during `Running` or `Unloading` prevents another dispatch but lets the current cycle finish before the phase becomes `Closed`. A breakdown during an active cycle follows the same completion behavior, then leaves the ride in `Broken`; a breakdown while waiting changes phase immediately. Served-guest totals update after unload, including when the ride was closed or broke during that cycle.
- Entrance/exit placement and operating configuration reject edits during `Running` or `Unloading`, even if the ride's state has already changed to `Closed` or `Broken`. Queue reconnection is additionally rejected while the ride state is `Open` (including `Waiting`), and rejects any proposed line whose internal-tile capacity is below retained occupancy, including after closing. Closing preserves occupancy, so a closed ride can only reconnect to a line with sufficient capacity. Queue reconnection otherwise becomes available after closing when no active cycle remains; a broken ride remains broken until inspected.
- `setRideOpen(..., false)` preserves the `Broken` ride state whenever either the stored state or phase is `Broken`. This also covers a breakdown during an active cycle: the ride state becomes broken immediately while its running or unloading phase finishes. `setRideOpen(..., true)` rejects broken rides. A valid mechanic inspection restores reliability and changes a broken ride to `Open`/`Waiting` only when its queue is connected; without a connected queue it becomes `Closed`/`Closed`.
- Inspection-due checks and manual mechanic inspection are hooks, not scheduled staff behavior. Inspection accepts a mechanic only when the ride is not in its running or unloading phase; it can be inspected while waiting or closed. Inspection restores up to 25 reliability points, capped at 100. Repair duration or staff travel is not simulated here.
- `recordRideTestMeasurements` validates and persists measurements but does not derive excitement, intensity, or nausea from track stats. Guests, pathfinding, staff automation, track testing, rendering, and ride-map occupancy remain integration responsibilities of their respective modules.
