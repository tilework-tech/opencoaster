# Noridoc: Scenario Simulation

Path: @/src/sim/scenario

### Overview

- Defines scenario starting finances, pricing, research, entrance placement and objective state, plus each scenario's generated map: terrain recipe, land ownership, tree and ground-scenery scatter, and pre-built rides.
- Validates scenario JSON and evaluates dated or open-ended objectives against the simulation.
- Keeps the active objective and win/loss status in `World` for deterministic saves and continuation.

### How it fits into the larger codebase

- The worker prepares new scenarios through [scenario-layout.ts](scenario-layout.ts), which applies scenario settings and builds the optional entrance path.
- [scenario-file.ts](scenario-file.ts) validates documents loaded by [catalog.ts](catalog.ts); errors identify the scenario and field, while duplicate IDs invalidate the catalog.
- [scenario-preview.ts](scenario-preview.ts) turns a prepared scenario `World` into a deterministic top-down, one-pixel-per-tile overview. The build-time generator uses the same scenario parser and map preparation path as a new game, so previews reflect each JSON map rather than a shared title-screen illustration.
- [scenario-system.ts](scenario-system.ts) runs after economy processing and reads current guest, ride, track, economy and ledger state when evaluating a deadline.
- [scenario.ts](scenario.ts) owns the flat objective record and terminal status in `World`; [save.ts](../../save/save.ts) preserves those fields without storing the source scenario document.
- In the final world-fidelity balance run, the default seed (2026) wins at park rating 474 against the 450 target after the trash rate was calibrated from one-in-48 to one-in-96. The earlier deferred-economy seed sweep is a separate baseline. See [docs/BALANCE.md](../../../docs/BALANCE.md#world-fidelity-pass) for the comparison and assumptions.

- [scenario-map.ts](scenario-map.ts) generates terrain when configured and scatters scenery from a scenario recipe; tree-only recipes can decorate the default map without replacing its seeded terrain. Starter Park configures a flat, owned apron 30 tiles wide by 20 tiles inward from the south entrance (600 tiles) at height 4, with surrounding terrain graded gently toward base height 2 and hills and a lake farther from the entrance. This gives new parks clear building space while keeping terrain features beyond the entrance area. The generator uses seeded placement and keeps scenery clear of the entrance apron and any configured gate-to-park corridor. [scenario-prebuilt.ts](scenario-prebuilt.ts) places standing rides; [park-grid.ts](park-grid.ts) is the gate-relative coordinate frame shared with the balance strategies in [bench/strategy](../../../bench/strategy).
- The shipped catalog is every `scenarios/*.json`: the starter plus ten more across the Beginner, Challenging, Expert, Real and Other tabs. Each tab's scenarios and objectives are listed with their scripted win rates in [docs/BALANCE.md](../../../docs/BALANCE.md#scenario-win-rates). Completion is remembered per browser by [scenario-progress.ts](../../save/scenario-progress.ts) and shown by the title screen.

### Core Implementation

- `generateScenarioMap` replaces the default seeded terrain only when a file has a `terrain` block. It layers coarse noise (`relief`, `cell`), `hills` and `lakes` over `baseLevel`, then fixes the apron at `buildLevel` and constrains surrounding vertices by distance so the level change returns gradually toward the configured terrain. The apron is `buildLength` tiles inward from the gate edge and `buildWidth` across, centred on the owned edge, and must fit inside the owned land. Water fills tiles below `seaLevel`; tiles at or below it are sand, the rest use `surface`. Tree-only recipes can still scatter scenery on the default map when no terrain recipe is present.
- `land.ownedMarginPercent` insets the owned rectangle (default 25, Starter Park's centre half); `land.saleMarginPercent` leaves an outer ring unsellable. [expansionOwnership](../map/tile-map.ts) marks the immediate ring around owned land `SALE_LAND`, while farther expansion tiles are `SALE_RIGHTS`, allowing the renderer and purchase UI to distinguish buying land from buying construction rights. The resulting ownership flags feed map buildability and are refreshed per tile during generation. `terrain.highland` turns ground at or above a level into a themed surface (dirt uplands, a rock mesa), and steep exposed faces become rock. `trees` configures tree and grove counts, with a share of solitary trees, on flat dry ground that suits each species: grass supports the configured tree species, sand supports palm and cactus, and rock takes none. Optional `shrubs` and `flowerBeds` add ground scenery; `clearWidth` is the half-width and `clearLength` the inward reach of a gate-aligned corridor reserved in addition to the apron and its two-tile margin. The generator is seeded from the world seed, so terrain and scenery placement are repeatable for a given seed.
- `prepareScenario` order: terrain, entrance (placed on the apron midpoint), standing rides, then `applyScenario`; a standing ride's `row`/`lateral` name its front corner (the footprint tile nearest the gate and the spine) and the footprint extends away from both, oriented to reach least across the spine (`rideFrontage` in [park-grid.ts](park-grid.ts)); construction of standing rides is free because cash and the month's ledger are reset afterwards. Standing rides use `prebuilt` entries (`type`, `row` inward from the gate, signed `lateral` across the spine of at least 2) and are opened with a queue, entrance and exit.
- The renderer draws its own ambient trees from tile coordinates (see [render docs](../../render/docs.md)); it selects palms or cacti on sand. Scenario trees, shrubs and flower beds are `SmallScenery` elements that participate in simulation scenery effects and are distinct from ambient rendering decorations.
- Scenario preview pixels start from terrain surface colours shaded by tile height, then apply water or ownership tints. Entrance, paths and queue paths, ride footprints or track, and scenery are drawn as tile markers above that base. Preview generation writes native-size and nearest-neighbour 2× PNGs plus source hashes; see [scenario preview generation](../../../tools/scenario-previews/docs.md).

- [scenario.ts](scenario.ts) defines objective kinds and the numeric objective record, and applies starting finance, price policy, research availability and objective state. Starter Park opens with the Merry-Go-Round, Swinging Ship, Ferris Wheel, Twist, Junior Roller Coaster, food and drink stalls, information kiosk and toilets available; its remaining rides stay in the research queue. Its objective remains 360 guests and a 540 park rating by the end of year one. [scenario-layout.ts](scenario-layout.ts) also places and registers an optional gate path.
- [scenario-file.ts](scenario-file.ts) validates bounded numeric fields, enums (including optional climate), income category masks, optional coaster thresholds and deadlines. It converts `byYear` to simulation ticks; a `byTick` deadline is used as supplied. Scenario JSON does not need to match an embedded interface shape beyond the fields the parser accepts.
- Scenario climate is applied to the world weather state, hashed and saved; scenarios that omit it use the warm default. The starter scenario declares that default explicitly.
- [scenario-system.ts](scenario-system.ts) judges dated objectives once, on the first tick at or after their deadline; the result is terminal. Families include guest count/rating, park value, loan repayment plus park value, monthly income, ride counts, qualifying or complete coasters, best-ride excitement and `HaveFun`. Aliases include `guestsByDate`, `parkValueByDate`, `monthlyRideIncome` and `monthlyFoodProfit`.
- `monthlyFoodProfit` uses net food/drink sales and stock costs. Kiosk and toilet revenue is recorded as general shop sales and does not satisfy that objective.
- Ride objectives scan ride and track stores at the evaluation tick. `QualifyingRides` counts open non-shop attractions; `BuildRides` counts current ride entities; `QualifyingCoasters` counts distinct open track-ride types meeting excitement and length thresholds; `CompleteCoasters` counts rides with a closed track and sufficient excitement; `BestRide` compares excitement for open rides. These criteria describe current state, not lifetime achievements.
- `MonthlyIncome` reads the most recently completed month. `Guests` may require a minimum park rating; `GuestsAndRating` requires both guest and rating thresholds; `RepayLoanAndParkValue` also requires zero loan. Park-value objectives use the economy's latest published park value input. `HaveFun` has no deadline and remains active.
- The [objective UI](../../ui/objective.ts) reads the objective and live metric. The park window shows the objective, active awards and current date; the HUD shows objective status and announces recent award news.

### Things to Know

- Park value includes the economy's valuation of closed non-shop rides and current guest count, but its ride inputs refresh periodically; an objective can therefore observe the most recently published value at its evaluation tick.
- Coaster length uses the track system's stored centre-line tile length. Objective field semantics and thresholds remain compatibility assumptions where checked-in mechanics sources do not establish original RCT2 behavior; see [docs/DECISIONS.md](../../../docs/DECISIONS.md).
- Entrance search starts at the owned-land edge midpoint and takes the first straight inward run of the requested length that is buildable, flat, dry and empty. If no run qualifies, no path or gate is placed.
