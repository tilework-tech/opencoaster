# Noridoc: Economy Simulation

Path: @/src/sim/economy

### Overview

- Owns the integer-cent economy, game calendar, monthly finance history, loans, wages, ride upkeep, marketing, park value and park rating inputs.
- Other simulation systems post transactions and casualty events through the economy API; recurring charges, monthly awards and periodic value/rating updates run in deterministic systems.
- Current mechanics and tuning are summarized in [economy.md](../../../docs/mechanics/economy.md), with binding choices in [DECISIONS.md](../../../docs/DECISIONS.md).

### How it fits into the larger codebase

- The worker-owned [World](../core/world.ts) contains the cash balance, ledger, calendar and published park inputs. World hashing and [save data](../../save/save.ts) preserve the economy state.
- Guest purchases, admissions, ride tickets and construction call [api.ts](api.ts) to post ledger entries. For example, a successful umbrella purchase at an information kiosk is recorded as `ShopSales` by the [guest system](../guests/guest-system.ts); unaffordable kiosks do not produce a transaction.
- [Park inputs](park-inputs.ts) derives wages, upkeep, ride value and guest/park metrics from current world stores. Its values are refreshed before the economy system processes shared tick boundaries; ride value reads recent ride rider windows from [ride-store.ts](../rides/ride-store.ts).
- [Simulation](../core/simulation.ts) applies economy actions from commands, while [economy-system.ts](economy-system.ts) advances the calendar and applies recurring charges and monthly rollover.
- [Worker snapshots](../../worker/snapshot.ts) expose active awards and bounded park news from the saved ledger. The park tab lists current awards and the formatted date; the [HUD](../../ui/game-ui.ts) announces recent award news.

### Core Implementation

- [api.ts](api.ts) is the entry point for earning and charging non-negative integer cents, publishing economy inputs, and querying admission price, ride pricing mode and marketing attraction. Invalid transaction amounts throw `RangeError`.
- [actions.ts](actions.ts) applies borrowing, repayment, pricing and marketing actions. Rejected actions return `false` without changing state.
- [calendar.ts](calendar.ts) defines the tick-based dates and boundary checks. [economy-system.ts](economy-system.ts) processes weekly wages, interest and campaign costs (research funding is also paid weekly in [research-system.ts](../research/research-system.ts), before the economy system), fortnightly ride upkeep, monthly ledger rollover, and periodic rating refreshes at those boundaries. On a shared boundary it posts weekly charges before fortnightly upkeep, then rolls the month and evaluates awards from refreshed park inputs.
- [ledger.ts](ledger.ts) defines transaction categories and rolling monthly totals. Guest kiosk umbrella revenue, like toilet fees, uses `ShopSales`; it is separate from food and drink sales.
- [park-inputs.ts](park-inputs.ts) sums role-specific staff wage rates from [staff-store.ts](../staff/staff-store.ts), plus ride and guest measures. It reads per-type prices, stock costs, generation bonuses and explicit running costs from the [ride catalog](../rides/ride-types.ts); the starter's seven ride, stall and facility entries each use $3.10 per fortnight. Types without explicit costs retain a local build-cost divisor fallback, whose basis remains unsettled against the compatibility reference. These inputs are republished periodically, so direct callers of `setEconomyInputs` should treat them as refreshed simulation inputs rather than durable overrides.
- [awards.ts](awards.ts) assesses candidate predicates after monthly rollover and the boundary's rating/value refresh, adds at most one eligible award per month up to four concurrent awards, and expires each after five months. Each live award changes the guest-generation probability by a quarter, up (positive) or down (negative), applied in sequence in [guest-generation.ts](guest-generation.ts). Food-award hunger counts use the guest stall-seeking cutoff (satiation 128 or lower); exact original award thresholds remain unverified. The water-ride candidate currently cannot qualify because the ride catalog has no water ride type.
- [guest-generation.ts](guest-generation.ts) turns the rating into arrivals, following the RCT2 model documented in [gap-audit.md](../../../docs/mechanics/gap-audit.md). On every rating refresh (512 ticks) the economy system caches `guestGeneration` on the ledger (saved and hashed): the per-tick arrival probability out of 65,536, the suggested guest maximum, and the ride value for money. The probability is `50 + clamp(rating - 200, 0, 650)`, quartered when the guests in the park exceed the suggested maximum (the sum of [ride-bonus-value.ts](../rides/ride-bonus-value.ts) over open, unbroken rides and stalls), quartered when the entrance fee exceeds the ride value for money (twice the summed value of open rides beyond their ticket price) and quartered again when half the fee still exceeds it, then raised or lowered by a quarter per live award. The guest system rolls against it once per tick and never refuses a guest outright. A running campaign adds a separate per-tick roll (`CampaignDef.guestsPerTick`: free entry 400, free rides 300, park advertising 250, half-price entry, free food and ride advertising 200; an eighth of that when the entry fee or targeted ride price is already low) and the guests it draws carry the campaign voucher. Unprompted arrivals carry none.
- Park value uses every non-shop ride, including closed rides. Each ride's contribution is `valueCents * (ridersLast5Minutes + 4 * typeBonus)`, using the age-adjusted `getRideValueCents` result (including its same-type reduction). These contributions are summed across rides, then 700 cents per current guest is added. Rider count is a five-minute rolling window. The type-bonus proxy is derived from the ride type's `valueWeights`; the formula and proxy remain compatibility assumptions documented in [park-rating.ts](park-rating.ts) and [DECISIONS.md](../../../docs/DECISIONS.md).
- Park rating includes separate persisted drowning and fatal-ride-crash penalties: 25 points per drowning and 200 per fatal ride crash, plus the generic legacy casualty input, with the combined penalty capped at 1,000. Each typed pool retains 90% each month with integer flooring; the pools do not reset or reduce one another. Multiplicative 10% monthly decay is an explicit assumption. The recording helpers currently have no simulation event caller, so neither casualty type is recorded by gameplay systems yet.
- `saveLedger` and `restoreLedger` in [ledger.ts](ledger.ts) form the ledger serialization boundary. New ledger fields must also be represented in [save data](../../save/save.ts) and [world hashing](../core/hash.ts).

### Things to Know

- Money uses integer cents. Cash may go below zero through recurring charges; construction actions check available cash before applying and post costs through the economy API. Ledger posting changes cash and monthly totals; it does not change the loan balance.
- Each food or drink sale posts its descriptor stock cost as a separate expense. Prices and stock amounts live with ride-type descriptors; see the [catalog](../rides/ride-types.ts) and [economy calibration crosswalk](../../../docs/fidelity/economy.md#economy-calibration-crosswalk). These are OpenRCT2 compatibility references where identified as such.
- The starter scenario selects pay-per-ride pricing and a $0 gate fee. Its flat-ride ticket defaults are $1.00 for the Merry-Go-Round, Ferris Wheel and Twist, and $1.50 for the Swinging Ship; food and drink start at $1.50 and $1.20 with separate $0.50 and $0.30 stock costs. These are selected OpenRCT2 compatibility references, not values specified by the RCT2 manual as universal defaults. The [scenario](../scenario/scenarios/starter.json) owns the pricing mode and admission fee; the [ride catalog](../rides/ride-types.ts) owns descriptor prices and stock costs.
- Staff salary constants use monthly amounts from the cited manual and convert them to weekly cents (a park month is four weeks, so a week is a quarter of the monthly salary); the ledger charges that weekly wage input at each week boundary, while monthly finance history aggregates the charges. Hiring and firing update the weekly input immediately, and park inputs recompute it from the roster on refresh. Ride upkeep remains a fortnightly charge. Research funding is paid a quarter of its monthly figure each week, so a completed month always holds the full monthly amount.
- [finance.ts](finance.ts) converts the configured annual interest rate into a weekly payment, samples the outstanding loan at the weekly boundary, and floors each payment separately. Interest is recorded as an expense without being added to principal. At $20,000 and 10% annual interest, the compatibility reference is $61.03 weekly or $244.12 over four weekly charges; see [economy fidelity notes](../../../docs/fidelity/economy.md). This conversion is distinct from the calendar's four-week financial month and eight-month park year.
- The park rating ([park-rating.ts](park-rating.ts)) is the RCT2 sum of named factors, exposed by `parkRatingBreakdown` and shown in the Park window's rating tab: base 1150 (1050 for difficult-rating scenarios); guests `guests/13 - 150` (guests capped at 2000); happiness `2 x min(250, 300 x happy / guests) - 500` (a guest is happy above 128); lost guests `-7` each beyond 25; ride uptime `2 x (100 - average downtime %) - 200` (no uptime credit with no rides); ride mix `-(min(50, |average excitement - 46| / 2) + min(50, |average intensity - 65| / 2))` in eighths of a hundredth, or `-100` with no rated ride; variety `(min(1000, excitement total) + min(1000, intensity total)) / 10 - 200`; litter `-4` per piece older than 7,680 ticks, up to 150 pieces; and casualties (capped at 1,000). Ride terms count every rated ride, open or not: a track ride once tested, a flat ride once it has base ratings. `park-inputs.ts` publishes `rideCount`, `ratedRideCount`, the eighths totals, `happyGuests` and the old-litter count; downtime is the share of rides and stalls currently broken, not RCT2's rolling window. An empty park therefore rates 0. Balance results and scenario targets are recorded in [docs/BALANCE.md](../../../docs/BALANCE.md). `restoreLedger` merges saved inputs and `guestGeneration` over defaults, so older ledger headers can omit newer inputs.
- Marketing has one active campaign slot, charged weekly while active. Six campaign families (ids 0-5, manual order) run for 1-6 weeks. Ride campaigns store a target ride; free food/drink vouchers store a target stall. The running campaign draws extra guests at its own per-tick chance out of 65,536 (`guestsPerTick` in [marketing.ts](marketing.ts); see [guest-generation.ts](guest-generation.ts)), separately from natural arrivals; each carries the campaign's voucher, if any. Entry vouchers affect the gate fee, and ride/food vouchers name the target they honour. Ride campaigns direct the guests they draw to the selected open ride. Campaign prices match the RCT2 weekly table. See [research-marketing.md](../../../docs/mechanics/research-marketing.md) and DECISIONS.md.
- Ride rider history uses a rolling window cleared in tick-sized segments and summed over the last five minutes. It is part of ride save state; version 12 adds the ring after version 11's ride columns, while older saves load the new columns as zero, as described in [save docs](../../save/docs.md).
- The calendar uses March–October and eight equal 16,384-tick months. Month day labels are display interpolation across 30- and 31-day month labels; tick boundaries, not labels, control financial processing. This schedule is an implementation compatibility assumption.
- Park news and active awards are deterministic ledger state, included in world hashing and saves. The UI presents recent items from the bounded news history.
- Borrowing and repayment rules are implemented in [finance.ts](finance.ts): loan changes use fixed $1,000 steps, repayment is immediate and limited by both the balance and available cash. Weekly interest is calculated from the balance at the boundary using the conversion described above. The ledger therefore reports interest payments separately from principal repayment.
